# Visual repertoire: findings and boundaries

The skill was evaluated with fictional presentation studies in Codex desktop on macOS. These are qualitative construction checks, not a ranking of presentation products or evidence that every catalog entry exports correctly.

## Findings that guide construction

| Finding | Practical response |
| --- | --- |
| A palette change can conceal repeated composition | Compare title placement, image geometry, density, chapter and closing behavior across decks |
| Immersive photo covers and identical dark closings narrowed an initial eight-deck study | Choose cover and closing structures from the story, including light editorial pages, typographic posters, photo bands and image-led endings |
| Family-level recipes blurred the difference between technical, cyberpunk and urban directions | Use [style grammars](../slidegenai/references/style-grammars.md) as optional composition-level examples |
| Generic type weakened character | Assign display/body/label roles, load available faces, then remeasure; use the [typography guide](../slidegenai/references/typography.md) and licensed local alternatives |
| Font substitutions changed optical scale and wrapping | Adjust the real frame/scale and inspect again instead of changing only the font name |
| Chart styling and flow routing can alter interpretation | Preserve source data, meaningful geometry, readable labels and attached endpoints where supported |
| A linear four-stage process can recur appropriately | Change topology when the content needs branching, feedback or dependencies; avoid decorative complexity without meaning |

## Rendered study evidence

An initial eight-deck study covered 64 slides with photographic/text layering, eleven native charts, eight native tables and attached process connectors. Every slide and overview was inspected; repairs addressed contrast, count ticks, proportional durations, chart outlines and connector labels.

The follow-up covered nine directions with six slides each: Acid Rave, Neo-Tech, Cyberpunk, NOVA, Ultimate Luxury, Neo Tokyo, Bauhaus, Kinetic and Corporate & Education. All 54 final slides and nine overviews were inspected after typography refinement. Seven native charts matched their declared series, including scatter x/y pairs; four native tables retained all declared cell values; 24 connector segments referenced endpoints on their own slide. A schedule retained proportional native geometry. Eighteen selected raster assets were generated concept imagery, not documentary photographs.

![Covers from nine fictional studies](previews/style-study-9.jpg)

The studies included distinct display voices, an editable native outlined word, contextual labels, photographic observation, editorial hierarchy and varied chapter/closing structures. Local proprietary system fonts used in those studies are not redistributed. Bundled open alternatives and suggested pairings are documented separately; they do not imply identical font metrics.

## What is distributed and what is verified

The contact sheet shows completed studies delivered as live editable artifacts; it is not a set of nine downloadable masters. The [eight-slide example](../examples/portable-deck/README.md), [analytical example](../examples/analytical-patterns/README.md) and their public adapters provide reproducible files. Their review records distinguish inspected artifacts from fresh builds and exporter limitations.

The 177-entry catalog is a source inventory with shared planning coverage. Only the recorded subset was visually evaluated. These checks do not establish animation support, font embedding, native editing in PowerPoint/Google Slides/Keynote, cross-provider equivalence, an aesthetic score or detection of AI authorship. See [validation](VALIDATION.md) and [manual evaluation](EVALUATION.md).
