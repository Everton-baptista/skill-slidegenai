# Quality review

Review the plan and the rendered artifact separately. This workflow does not introduce extra approval gates for work already authorized.

## Identity

For original work, check consistency with the authored concept, palette, typography and layout relationships; do not report a missing brand guide or logo as an error. When a reference exists, compare against it: correct brand/sub-brand, source version, logo variant, proportions, clear space when evidenced, background, palette roles, and typography. Record unavailable fonts and substitutions. Do not claim official compliance based only on a plausible visual resemblance.

## Narrative and facts

Check total count, order, outline coverage, meaningful topic covers, names, figures, sources, and qualifications. Remove generic filler. Ensure illustrative data is visibly identified and factual claims have real support. Verify source content rather than merely checking that a citation string exists.

## Composition

Inspect every slide at reading size and the overview for rhythm. Check clipped text, awkward wrapping, safe margins, density, collisions, image crops, missing glyphs, chart labels, legends, and meaningful hierarchy. Important information should not depend only on color.

For solid backgrounds, [WCAG contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) provides useful reference ratios of 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for qualifying large text. This does not certify a slide deck. The helper warns below 4.5:1 regardless of size; it does not classify large text or inspect transparency, images, gradients, or actual font rendering. Inspect those cases manually.

## Authored composition

Use the critique in [art direction](art-direction.md) for new directions or creative adaptations. Inspect reading order, specific image contribution, line breaks, deliberate empty areas, and deck-level cadence. Remove generic filler and unsupported “human” annotations. An organic gesture must serve the subject and survive export; do not add randomness to satisfy an originality claim.

## Brief-specific visual completeness

For an expressive or image-led request, inspect whether the intended visual material actually appears. Check the cover's focal relationship, readable content pages and a meaningful change of pace across the overview. A palette change or one accent-colored page does not by itself demonstrate the intended art direction. When the brief intentionally calls for restrained typography, judge its hierarchy and composition instead of adding compulsory pictures.

If an acquired/generated asset was omitted, determine whether the content or tool limit justifies that omission. Repair an accidental omission before delivery. Name a real capability gap when it changes the requested result; do not offer to add images later when the current request and tools already support them.

## Images and export

Check actual image resolution, provider branding/watermarks, provenance, and allowed use. Ensure downloaded assets are present and external references do not silently disappear. Inspect the exported PPTX/PDF itself for substitutions, SVG differences, lost overlays, transparency, and layer order. A source preview alone is insufficient. For full-canvas photographs, inspect each reading region over the real image, including gradient direction/alpha, faces beneath text, and logos/page numbers on bright details. Check typographic symbols as well as words.

## Verify edits before handing them back

After inserting or moving a title, illustration, logo or image, inspect the affected slide with the available render, preview, screenshot or exported-file tool. Check its actual boundaries and nearby text. Do not replace this step with “confirm it does not touch the title” when the environment offers inspection.

If no visual inspection path exists, say which capability is unavailable and identify the affected slide as unverified. Geometry checks can reduce obvious collisions but do not prove font wrapping, stroke bounds, crops or appearance. Do not claim that an unverified change is visually complete. Keep material limitations concise and continue work that remains possible.

## Delivery record

Report the output path/format, slide count, reference used or original direction, checks actually completed, editing limitations, substitutions, and unresolved issues. Call outputs without final visual inspection unverified drafts. Do not mark self-reported review fields true unless the inspection happened.

Optional structural checks are useful early and after repairs. Their success is not evidence of design quality, accessibility certification, factual accuracy, or asset licensing.
