# Lessons extracted from SlideGenAI

These are portable observations from the SlideGenAI source reviewed for this skill, not dependencies on that application. No application implementation is bundled.

| Observed pattern | Keep | Adapt carefully |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Insper institutional content/masters separation | Stable geometry, real identity, topic-cover continuity, protected photo text | Do not transfer its red/charcoal palette, photo requirements, or text budgets to other brands |
| Organic Pro's two prescribed structures | Measurable regions and predictable content fit | Do not force every idea into the same bento/split-mask layout |
| Organic Modern and Wellness Organic | Attention to softer cadence, cropping, tone | Organic meaning must not collapse into blobs, pastel circles, botanical photos, or round cards on every slide |
| Editorial, magazine, modern zine | Deliberate scale, photography, typography, asymmetry | A cultural essay and a financial report need different editorial decisions; no automatic enormous serif headings |
| Watercolor, sketch, material/nature themes | A medium can support a subject's character | Use appropriate source imagery; simulated human marks and random texture do not prove authenticity |
| Rendered overflow detection | Inspect nested clipping and measure after fonts load | Valid boxes or a template name do not guarantee rendered readability |
| Photo/provider fallbacks in previews | Check actual assets before use | Do not copy remote fallback URLs or assume a no-logo parameter removes current service branding |

The resulting skill separates identity, measured construction, and content, then adds a small art-direction decision process. Templates are a source of tested visual relationships, not a finite menu that limits every new deck.

The repository includes a controlled three-direction study. The same source proposition is treated as analytical, editorial, and organic when the audience/context permits. It is not a universal style selector or evidence of cross-provider quality.
